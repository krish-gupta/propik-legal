Privacy Policy

PROP PIK Capture for iPhone. Effective date: 21 November 2026.

PROP PIK Capture ("the app") helps you take, view and organise 360° photos with your Insta360 camera. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: your photos and data stay on your iPhone. The app has no account, no advertising and no tracking, and it does not send your data to us.

Information the app uses

  • Photos you take. Photos from your camera are copied to your iPhone and stored inside the app. We cannot see them.
  • Location and compass direction. With your permission, the app saves the GPS position and compass direction of each photo, so you can see where it was taken and which way is north. This information is stored with the photo on your iPhone only.
  • Photos you import. If you choose photos from your Photo Library to import, only the photos you pick are copied into the app.
  • Motion sensors. Used only so you can look around a 360° photo or the live view by moving your phone. Motion data is not stored or sent.
  • Bluetooth, local network and Wi-Fi. Used only to find and talk to your Insta360 camera over its own Wi-Fi, for the live view and to copy photos to your phone.
  • iPhone camera and microphone. The app does not use them. You will not be asked for access to either.
  • Your name. If you type a name in Settings, it is saved on your iPhone and added to the projects and exports you create.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect your personal information on any server of ours.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and we do not sell or share your data.
  • We do not require an account.
  • Crash reporting is switched off in this version.

Sharing

The app shares a photo or a ZIP export only when you choose to, using the iOS share sheet. Where it goes then is up to you and the app you choose.

Your camera

Your Insta360 camera is a separate product. The app talks to it using Insta360's camera software library. Please see Insta360's own privacy policy for how the camera and its own app handle data.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your photos and settings stay on your iPhone until you delete them. You can delete a photo inside the app (and, if you choose, from the camera's memory card too). Deleting the app removes all of its data from your iPhone.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy (for example, if accounts or online features are added), we will update this page and the effective date above before the change is released.
