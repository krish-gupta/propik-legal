PROP PIK Capture

Support

PROP PIK Capture is an iPhone app for taking, viewing and organising 360° photos with an Insta360 camera (X3, X4 and X5).

Need help?

Email guptakms@gmail.com. Please tell us your iPhone model, your camera model and what happened. We aim to reply within a few days.

Quick tips

  • Connecting the camera: switch the camera on, allow Bluetooth and Local Network when the app asks, and let the app join the camera's Wi-Fi.
  • No camera? Try the app with the demo camera: Settings > Camera > Capture method > Demo camera.
  • Deleting photos: choose "Delete from iPhone and camera" to remove the photo from both.
